Before you go

Look at you, all ready for your little adventure. Oso is rooting for you.

A 6K may not need an energy gel at all. A familiar carb rich dinner is plenty, no need to overdo it. Safety and comfort matter more than pace.

A little prayer

If the road gets hard

The little tune at the bottom is an original instrumental made right in your browser. It borrows its mood from the big sky feeling of Coldplay and the warm soul of Olivia Dean, but it is not their music.

If you feel tired

Slow down or walk. You don’t need to prove anything to anyone. If you feel dizzy, have chest pain, or feel unusually unwell, stop and seek help.

If you get nervous

Take a few slow breaths, relax your shoulders, and focus on the next small step instead of the whole 6K.

If you start comparing yourself

Remember that this is your first 6K. Other people’s pace is their story. This one is yours.

If you finish

Smile, breathe, drink some water, and give yourself credit. You did something you hadn’t done before.

A few quiet notes

Wear shoes and clothes you have already run in. Race day is not the time to try anything new.
Start slower than feels necessary. The first kilometer should feel easy.
Walking breaks are part of running. Many first time runners use them.
